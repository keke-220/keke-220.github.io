Xiaohan Zhang

I am currently a robotics researcher at Boston Dynamics AI Institute.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the State University of New York at Binghamton, advised by Shiqi Zhang. In the summer and fall of 2023, I was a Research Scientist Intern in the Embodied AI team at Meta AI (FAIR) working with Chris Paxton. In the spring of 2023, I spent time visiting the Learning Agents Research Group (LARG) at the University of Texas at Austin, supervised by Peter Stone. In the summer of 2022, I was a Student Researcher with the Robotics team at Google DeepMind. I obtained my bachelor's degree from Renmin University of China in 2019.
